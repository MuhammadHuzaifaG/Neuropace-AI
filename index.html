Optimal Daily Targets for Health & Peace of Mind

To maintain long-term physical health, mental calm, and cognitive balance, aim to keep your daily health metrics within these target ranges:

    Optimal Daily Targets

  • Symptom Severity (Aim for 1–3): Keep discomfort at a minimum. If your severity rating climbs above 5, lower your screen exposure and take a visual break immediately.
  • Sleep Quality (Aim for 8–10): Restorative sleep is the baseline for neural recovery. Aim for 7 to 9 hours of deep, uninterrupted sleep each night to keep your mental stamina high.
  • Physical Activity (Aim for 30–60 mins): Light-to-moderate movement (like walking, gentle stretching, or yoga) promotes blood flow and releases tension without triggering cognitive fatigue.
  • Screen Time (Limit to 2–4 Hours non-work): Prolonged visual strain elevates anxiety and eye fatigue. Take regular 20-second breaks by looking at distant objects across the room.
  • Hydration & Mindfulness (Daily Baseline): Drink plenty of water and take 2 to 5 minutes daily for targeted visual tracking or breathing exercises to restore your nervous system.
💡 Remember: Consistency over intensity. Small, steady daily habits lead to sustained energy, clearer focus, and lasting peace of mind.

Duration & Frequency

2 to 5 minutes per session is all you need. If you're new to visual exercises, start with 2 minutes once a day. As your eyes adjust, you can gradually work up to a 5-minute session or split it into two short daily sessions.

Listen to Your Body

Therapy should feel restorative, not punishing. If you feel dizzy, lightheaded, or experience mild strain:

  • Click Pause at any point to rest your eyes.
  • Look at a distant object across the room for 20 seconds.
  • Resume only when you feel completely comfortable.
Why This Helps: Smooth visual tracking stimulates the connection between your optical nerves and brain, helping to reduce physical tension, improve eye stamina, and support a calmer nervous system over time.

Track your daily metrics and let AI predict when you need a brain break. Spending just 1 to 2 minutes logging your daily health habits helps customize your mental, physical, and visual recovery plan. Here is how to get the best results:

Step-by-Step Guide

  • Review Your Recommendations: Read your personalized Patient Guidance for instant pacing, and recovery tips, alongside a structured.
  • Save Your Progress: Click the "Save Entry" button to retain your historical trends.

2.Oculomotor Therapy - Eye Tracking Exercise

Welcome to your visual therapy space. Taking just 2 to 5 minutes out of your day can make a significant difference in your focus, eye tracking, and mental calm. Here is how to get the best results:

Step-by-Step Guide

  • Find Your Center: Sit in a comfortable, upright chair in a quiet space with soft lighting. Position your screen at roughly an arm's length away.
  • Keep Your Head Still: As the target moves, follow it strictly with your eyes. Try not to tilt or turn your head—let your eye muscles do all the work.
  • Start When Ready: Press the "Begin Exercise" button below. Focus smoothly on the moving object without forcing or straining your vision.
  • Breathe and Blink: It is easy to hold your breath or freeze your focus! Remind yourself to take deep, relaxed breaths and blink naturally to keep your eyes hydrated.

3. Interactive Neuro-Somatic Bio-Pacing Engine

Regulate autonomic strain, visual fatigue, and mental overwhelm in real time. Spending just 1 to 2 minutes executing targeted neuro-somatic pacing routines helps realign your nervous system and prevent cognitive crashes. Here is how to get the best results:

Step-by-Step Guide

  • Identify Your Acute Symptom: Select a specialized exercise based on your immediate need—whether it is Vagus Nerve Reset for stress, Oculomotor Shift for eye strain, Orthostatic Pacing for physical fatigue, Bilateral Tapping for mental overwhelm, Rib Expansion for low oxygenation, or 4-7-8 Grounding for anxiety.
  • Launch Live Pacing: Click your chosen exercise button to initiate the interactive real-time visual pacing ring and automated countdown timer.
  • Follow Somatic Cues: Sync your breathing, eye movements, or physical posture with the dynamic visual ring transitions and step-by-step phase indicators.
  • Read Clinical Insights: Review the live Clinical Guidance box attached to each protocol to understand the physiological mechanisms supporting your recovery.

About NeuroPace AI

What is NeuroPace AI?

NeuroPace AI is an innovative web-based healthcare companion designed specifically for concussion recovery. It combines artificial intelligence, eye-tracking therapy, and symptom monitoring to support your recovery journey with science-backed techniques.

Key Features

  • Cognitive Pacing Tracker: AI-powered predictions help you understand when your brain needs rest, preventing overexertion during recovery.
  • Oculomotor Therapy: Interactive eye-tracking exercises that help retrain visual focus and coordination, addressing common post-concussion visual symptoms.
  • Data Visualization: Track trends in your symptoms over time with beautiful, accessible charts.
  • Accessible Design: Low-blue-light dark theme reduces cognitive load and protects sensitive eyes during recovery.

Disclaimer

NeuroPace AI is designed as a supportive tool and should not replace professional medical advice. Always consult with your healthcare provider, physical therapist, or concussion specialist before starting any new exercises or therapy. If you experience severe symptoms, dizziness, or worsening vision during therapy, stop immediately and seek medical attention.
